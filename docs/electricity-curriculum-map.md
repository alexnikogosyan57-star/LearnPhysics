## Independent model references and limits

- Coulomb point-charge law, k = 8.9875517923×10⁹ N·m²/C². The elementary charge e = 1.602176634×10⁻¹⁹ C is defined by SI. Electron-cloud graphics are schematic, not classical orbits or quantum simulation.
- DC: one ideal voltage source, positive resistances, ideal switches. Ideal ammeters are zero-voltage constraints in modified nodal analysis; infinite-resistance voltmeters report actual node-potential differences. Floating probes do not get invented values. Shorts, unsupported component types and indeterminate ideal-meter loops are rejected.
- Daniell cell: Zn/Zn²⁺ and Cu²⁺/Cu, −0.76 V and +0.34 V versus SHE, 298 K and unit activities. Only this electrode/solution pair is supported; internal resistance is neglected. Electron, conventional-current and ionic transport directions are distinguished. Directional arrows do not imply real carrier speed.
- Electrochemical reference: https://chem.libretexts.org/Bookshelves/General_Chemistry/Chemistry_2e_(OpenStax)/17%3A_Electrochemistry/17.03%3A_Electrode_and_Cell_Potentials
